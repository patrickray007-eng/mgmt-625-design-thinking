# Song Split Sheet

> Fill one out and get it **signed by everyone** *before* release — ideally in the
> studio the day the song is finished. A signed split sheet is your single best
> protection against royalty disputes, and it's what lets you register correct
> splits at BMI, The MLC, and with your distributor. Splits total **100%** in each
> column. Keep the signed copy; enter the numbers into `Splits-Register.csv`.

**A song has TWO ownership layers — fill in both:**
- **Composition / Songwriting** (the melody + lyrics) → drives **BMI performance**
  and **MLC mechanical** royalties.
- **Master / Recording** (this specific recording) → drives **DistroKid streaming**
  and **SoundExchange** royalties.

They're often the same people at different percentages (e.g., a producer may get
master points but no songwriting share, or vice versa). Be explicit.
